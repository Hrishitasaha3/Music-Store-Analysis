# Music Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes music store sales data using MySQL and Power BI to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, and revenue performance across genres, artists, and countries.

## Tools Used
- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench
- Power BI
- DAX

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Exploration:** Explored the Chinook database and its tables, relationships, and structure.
2. **SQL Analysis:** Used SQL queries, joins, aggregations, CTEs, and window functions to analyze sales, customers, artists, genres, and revenue.
3. **Data Validation:** Verified key revenue calculations between SQL and Power BI.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard using KPIs, charts, and slicers.
5. **Business Insights:** Identified key sales trends, top-performing markets, artists, genres, tracks, and customer patterns.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall revenue and sales performance.
- Identify top-performing artists and music genres.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Explore sales performance across countries.
- Examine monthly and annual revenue trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard to visualize business insights.

## SQL Analysis
SQL was used to perform data exploration and business analysis, including:
- Revenue analysis
- Country-wise sales analysis
- Customer spending analysis
- Artist and genre revenue analysis
- Ranking analysis using window functions
- Revenue contribution analysis

## Key Business Insights
1. **Dominant Market:** The United States generates the highest revenue among all billing countries, highlighting it as the store's strongest market.
2. **Genre Performance:** Rock is the leading revenue-generating genre, indicating strong customer demand for rock music.
3. **Artist Performance:** Iron Maiden leads artist revenue, followed by U2 and Metallica, making them important contributors to store sales.
4. **Track Performance:** Tracks such as "Dazed and Confused" and "The Trooper" rank among the highest revenue-generating tracks.
5. **Customer Contribution:** A subset of customers contributes higher individual sales, presenting opportunities for customer retention and personalized promotions.
6. **Monthly Sales Variation:** Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year, suggesting potential opportunities for targeted promotions during lower-performing periods.
7. **Annual Revenue Performance:** Annual revenue appears relatively stable across the years displayed, allowing the business to examine year-to-year performance patterns.
8. **Average Order Value:** The average order value is approximately 5.65, providing a baseline for strategies aimed at increasing spending per transaction.
